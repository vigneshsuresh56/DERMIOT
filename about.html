The Genesis

Care Beyond Boundaries Born From Living Earth

DERMIQT was founded in 2020 by botanist Dr. Elena Vance after spending over a decade researching indigenous herbal pharmacology. She witnessed how conventional cosmetics, laden with silicones, parabens, and aggressive petrochemical emulsifiers, chronically stripped the skin's protective moisture barrier.

We set out with an unyielding promise: to construct holistic skin rituals purely from organic cold-pressed plants, therapeutic clays, and nutrient-dense lipids. We refuse filler water, synthetic preservatives, and artificial fragrances. Every formula is designed to mimic your skin’s biological lipid architecture.
